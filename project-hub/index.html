    📝 Design Decisions¶

    Why Two-Tier Project Management?¶

    Decision: Adopt .STATUS + PROJECT-HUB.md system from zsh-configuration

    Rationale: - Prevents cognitive overload (don't think strategy when doing tasks) - Enables quick context recovery (< 5 seconds) - Separates tactical (today) from strategic (this week/month) - ADHD-friendly: Right level of detail for right purpose

    Trade-offs: - ✅ Pro: Clear separation of concerns - ✅ Pro: Faster daily updates - ✅ Pro: Better strategic planning - ⚠️ Con: Two files to maintain (but different cadences)

    Why Not Build Software?¶

    Decision: Keep Nexus as documentation/template, not installable software

    Rationale: - Users need to customize for their domain - Obsidian already provides the platform - Templates more flexible than rigid software - Lower maintenance burden - Easier to fork and adapt

    Trade-offs: - ✅ Pro: Infinitely customizable - ✅ Pro: No dependency management - ✅ Pro: Works with any Obsidian version - ⚠️ Con: Requires manual setup - ⚠️ Con: No "one-click install"

    Why PARA Method?¶

    Decision: Use PARA (Projects, Areas, Resources, Archives) for folder structure

    Rationale: - Industry-standard knowledge management framework - Clear separation of active vs reference material - ADHD-friendly (reduces decision fatigue) - Scales well (works for 10 or 1000 notes) - Well-documented methodology

    Trade-offs: - ✅ Pro: Familiar to PKM community - ✅ Pro: Clear organizational logic - ✅ Pro: Reduces "where does this go?" paralysis - ⚠️ Con: Some items could fit multiple categories - ⚠️ Con: Requires discipline to maintain
