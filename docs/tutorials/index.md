## FAQ

??? question "How long until Nexus feels natural?"
    About 2 weeks. Days 1-3 feel deliberate, by week 2 the morning brief and capture become automatic.

??? question "Do I need to read all tutorials first?"
    No. Read [Big Picture](big-picture.md), do [Setup](first-day.md), then learn workflows as you need them.

??? question "What if I miss days?"
    No problem. Say "Catch-up review" and Claude adapts. No guilt required.

??? question "Can I customize everything?"
    Yes — templates, commands, and workflows are all adjustable. Start with defaults, change what doesn't fit.
